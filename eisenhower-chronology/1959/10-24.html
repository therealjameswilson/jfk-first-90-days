FROM: SAC, MIAMI (105- )

CARLOS HILARIO GONZALEZ

IS - CUBA; RA - CUBA

Enclosed are 11 copies of a letterhead memorandum concerning an alleged plot by the subject to kill ESTEBAN VENTURA NOVO. The source of the information in enclosed memo is ANA ROSA GUERRA, who contacted this office on 10/24/59 and advised she works for BERNARD L BARKER, in an undercover capacity in CUBA where she is employed as secretary to ALBERTO BAYO COSGAYA, Chief of Air Fumigation-Technical, Cuban Air Force.
