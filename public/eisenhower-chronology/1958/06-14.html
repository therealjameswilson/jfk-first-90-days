CIA · Document date not identified · Matched: 14 June 1958 · Source: 104-10226-10045.md · NARA release

Excerpt (+/-300 characters around match):

WH/4/CI, Clark Simmons, with Mr. Cleveland C. Cram and John C. Roberts of WB/6 also present.

2. At the outset Mr. Oldfield provided the following information:

a. Robert Morten Geddes is a British subject born in Lima, Peru, on 7 April 1932. He holds British passport No. C-34880, issued at Nasagua on 14 June 1958. He was not registered at the British Consulate in Havana and was regarded as being American or Canadian.

b. Two employees of the British Embassy, Havana, has visited Geddes in the Havana jail (military G-2 section) on Sunday evening, 30 April 1961, and had a 15-minute conversation with Geddes alone
