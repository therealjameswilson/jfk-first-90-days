Promotion - Newton Scott Miler

1. It is recommended that Newton Scott Miler be promoted from his present grade of GS-14 to GS-15. Miler was assigned to the Africa Division on 9 July 1961 and assumed his present position of Chief of Station, Addis Ababa, in September 1961. He has been in grade since 24 March 1957. In accepting his present assignment Miler assumed responsibility for a very complicated operational program requiring considerable reorientation and modification. In a short time he has been able to give purpose and direction to operations which previously had been the subject of considerable controversy
