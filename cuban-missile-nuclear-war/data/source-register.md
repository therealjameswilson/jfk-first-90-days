# Cuban Missile Crisis Nuclear War Primary-Source Repository

This register compiles publicly available primary documents and authoritative archival listings that bear directly on what would have happened if the Cuban Missile Crisis escalated into U.S.-Soviet nuclear war. Inclusion is limited to documents that show nuclear war plans, casualty or target assumptions, U.S. airstrike or invasion paths, Soviet and Cuban nuclear-use authority, flashpoints that could have triggered nuclear use, or immediate post-crisis evidence about nuclear weapons remaining in Cuba.

Coverage note: The register is a public-document repository, not a claim that every still-classified or undigitized archival record has been located. Records marked as archival-listing are primary documents identified in official FRUS microfiche source tables but not linked to a public scan here.
